What Often Gives a Deepfake Away

Common tells in synthetic audio -- treat these as a caution, not a guarantee:

  • Unnaturally even pacing, with none of the small pauses, restarts, or "um"s of natural speech.
  • A flat or mismatched emotional tone -- urgency in the words, but calm in the delivery.
  • Missing background sound: no breathing, no room noise, no mouth or lip sounds.
  • A slightly metallic, warbly, or inconsistent audio quality within the same clip.
  • Odd emphasis on the wrong word, or stiff transitions between sentences.

As these tools improve, some fakes will sound completely convincing. That's exactly why the rule is PAUSE, THINK, REPORT -- not "listen closely enough."

Voice is not proof of identity.
