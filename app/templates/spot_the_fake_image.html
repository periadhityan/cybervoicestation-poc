What Often Gives a Deepfake Photo Away

Common tells in AI-generated or manipulated images -- treat these as a caution, not a guarantee:

  • Hands, ears, teeth, or glasses that look slightly wrong, asymmetric, or malformed.
  • Skin that's unnaturally smooth, or hair that blurs strangely into the background.
  • Reflections, shadows, or lighting that don't match the rest of the scene.
  • Backgrounds with warped text, odd patterns, or objects that don't quite make sense.
  • An overall quality mismatch between the subject and the background.

As these tools improve, some fakes will look completely convincing. That's exactly why the rule is PAUSE, THINK, REPORT -- not "look closely enough."
